# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html), version 2.1.

We expect participants to be respectful, direct, and constructive. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, trolling, and
publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove or reject contributions that violate this code.
They will explain moderation decisions when appropriate and will protect the
privacy of people who report a concern.

Report a conduct concern privately to [@IanHollow](https://github.com/IanHollow).
Do not use a public issue for a sensitive report. Maintainers will review reports
fairly and take proportionate action, including a warning, temporary ban, or
permanent ban from project spaces.
